In 2024, a 22 years old genius called Leopold Aschenbrenner published his thesis about the next 10 years in AI. So far, he got almost everything right. His hedge fund, Situational Awareness is up over 1,000%. He is now a billionaire (with net worth ~ $4 billion) and made billions for others (see Situational Awareness LP's latest public 13F filing).

Yet, since 2025, Leopold has gone completely silent. He hasn't posted on X or on his blog. One rare piece of information that got out is that he got engaged to the Chief of Staff at Anthropic, Avital Balwit. Silicon Valley is a small world.

I'm sure many people, including myself, would love to read an updated version of Leopold's thesis. The one _certain_ thing today is that we are at a pivotal moment in history. In 2034, nobody will be looking back at the past decade saying: _"Except for my iPhone 24, not much has changed since 2024"_.

In my quest to predict the future, I stumbled across a new thesis of a new fund that reads quite similar to Situational Awareness. Meet (Sigil Supernova Memo)[https://www.sigilfund.com/blog/sigil-supernova-memo].  In contrast to Situational Awareness's urgent 2024 manifesto forecasting aggressive AGI timelines by 2027, an intelligence explosion to superintelligence, and associated national-security/alignment risks, it presents a more pragmatic, post-LLM investment view focused on current physical-world bottlenecks, diversified opportunities across datacenters, robotics, space, biotech, and defense where capital owners and incumbents win.

I wholely recommend reading both thesis!
